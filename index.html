Neighborhoods across Tampa Bay and Sarasota

  • South Tampa

    Hyde Park's brick streets and Bayshore Boulevard's waterfront walk, with the shops and restaurants of Hyde Park Village close enough to stroll to. Long the most sought-after side of the city.

    Walkable · Historic · Waterfront

  • Seminole Heights

    Craftsman bungalows under old oaks, and a genuinely independent food and brewery scene. Minutes from downtown without downtown density.

    Bungalows · Food scene · Near downtown

  • Channel District & Water Street

    Tampa's newest downtown quarter — high-rise condos, the Riverwalk, Amalie Arena and the Water Street build-out. Walkable in a way not much else in the city is.

    Condos · Walkable · Downtown

  • Carrollwood

    Established north Tampa built around its lakes, with mature trees and quick access down Dale Mabry. Convenient without being new construction.

    Established · Lakes · Convenient

  • Westchase

    A planned community of sidewalks, parks and golf on the northwest edge of the county, with a straight run into town on the Veterans Expressway.

    Family-friendly · Planned · Golf

  • New Tampa

    Newer construction and larger lots up around Tampa Palms, with plenty of green space and quick access to I-75.

    Newer builds · More space · Parks

  • Wesley Chapel

    The growth corridor north of the county line, where new subdivisions, schools and retail keep arriving along I-75. A lot of the region’s newest inventory is here.

    New builds · Growing · Value

  • Brandon & Valrico

    Established suburbs east of the city, with lots getting bigger as you move out toward Valrico, and easy access to I-75 and the Selmon Expressway.

    Suburban · Bigger lots · Commuter

  • Riverview & Apollo Beach

    More house for the money southeast of the city, much of it recently built, with marina access down toward the bay.

    Newer builds · Space · Marinas

  • Downtown St. Petersburg

    Museums, murals and a waterfront park system, wrapped around a walkable core that keeps getting denser. A different rhythm from Tampa proper.

    Arts · Waterfront · Walkable

  • Safety Harbor & Clearwater

    Small-town main street on the Pinellas side, with the Gulf beaches a short drive west. Quieter, and close to the water.

    Small-town · Near beaches

  • Dunedin & Palm Harbor

    A walkable downtown of breweries and cafes on the Pinellas Trail, with the causeway out to Honeymoon Island. Unhurried, and very much its own place.

    Walkable · Trail · Near beaches

  • Tarpon Springs

    Greek heritage and the sponge docks at the top of Pinellas, with waterfront homes tucked along the bayous.

    Historic · Waterfront

  • Lakewood Ranch

    One of the largest master-planned communities in the country, spread across the Manatee–Sarasota line. Villages, golf, parks and schools inside the community, with new construction still going up.

    Master-planned · Golf · Family-friendly

  • Bradenton & Anna Maria Island

    An old-Florida river town on the Manatee, with the low-rise, no-chain charm of Anna Maria Island out at the Gulf end.

    Old Florida · Island · Waterfront

  • Sarasota

    An arts town with a serious cultural calendar, barrier-island beaches at Lido and Siesta Key, and a downtown that stays busy year-round.

    Arts · Beaches · Walkable

Landmarks and day trips

  • Armature Works

    A restored streetcar barn on the river, now a food hall with a rooftop bar and a lawn out front. Weekend brunch here is a Tampa institution.

    Food hall · Riverfront

  • Tampa Riverwalk

    A continuous waterfront path linking parks, museums and restaurants along the Hillsborough River through the middle of downtown.

    Walk · Parks · Museums

  • Ybor City

    Tampa's historic Latin quarter - cigar factories turned into restaurants and music venues, brick streets, and resident roosters.

    Historic · Nightlife

  • Busch Gardens

    Roller coasters and a genuine zoo on the same ticket, right inside the city. Annual passes are common enough here that families treat it as a regular Saturday.

    Theme park · Families

  • Raymond James Stadium

    Home of the Buccaneers and the stadium concert circuit, with the Lightning playing downtown at Amalie Arena and the Rays across the bay.

    Sports · Concerts

  • Tampa International Airport

    Consistently one of the easier large airports in the country to get through, and close enough to most of the city to be a genuine selling point.

    Travel · Convenience

  • Honeymoon Island State Park

    Undeveloped Gulf shoreline, pine trails and a ferry across to Caladesi Island, just over the causeway from Dunedin.

    State park · Beach · Trails

  • Clearwater Beach

    Wide sugar-white sand and the Gulf sunsets people move here for, roughly a 45-minute drive west from Tampa.

    Beach · Sunsets

  • St. Pete Beach & Pass-a-Grille

    Laid-back Gulf beaches, the landmark pink Don CeSar, and the quiet old-Florida end of the island at Pass-a-Grille.

    Beach · Old Florida

  • The Dalí Museum

    One of the largest collections of Salvador Dalí’s work anywhere, in a building on the St. Pete waterfront that is worth the trip by itself.

    Museum · Waterfront

  • Fort De Soto Park

    A county park spread over five keys at the mouth of the bay — swimming beaches, kayak trails, camping and a genuine 19th-century fort.

    Park · Beach · Camping

  • Anna Maria Island

    Seven miles of low-rise barrier island with a free trolley running end to end, beach cottages instead of towers, and no chain restaurants in sight.

    Beach · Old Florida · Family day

  • Lido Key & St. Armands Circle

    Beach in the morning, then shops and dinner around St. Armands Circle in the evening — the two are a few minutes apart.

    Beach · Shopping · Sarasota

  • The Ringling

    The state art museum of Florida on a bayfront estate in Sarasota, with the circus museum and Ca’ d’Zan mansion on the same grounds.

    Museum · Historic · Gardens

  • Siesta Key

    Quartz sand so fine it stays cool underfoot, just off Sarasota, and regularly ranked among the best beaches in the country.

    Beach · Sarasota

  • Walt Disney World

    About ninety minutes northeast, which puts a Disney day trip comfortably within reach — one of the quieter perks of living on this side of the state.

    Day trip · Orlando · Families
