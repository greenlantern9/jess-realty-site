The process

From "should we?" to "we're home."

01

Consult

We sit down — coffee, phone, or video — and map your goals, timeline, and budget. No commitment, just a clear plan and honest numbers.

02

Prep & price

Selling, we stage, shoot, and price to win. Buying, I get you fully pre-approved and ready to move the moment the right home appears.

03

Go to market

Your listing hits every channel that matters, or I surface homes before they're public. Either way, we move with intention.

04

Negotiate

This is where the right agent earns their keep. I protect your price, your terms, and your peace of mind through every counter.

05

Close & celebrate

Inspections, appraisal, paperwork — I quarterback it all so closing day is the easy part. Then I hand you the keys.
